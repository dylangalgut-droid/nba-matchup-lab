02 / HOW MATCHUPS ARE SCORED

Context beyond raw position

Minutes and injury effects are estimates, not confirmed lineups. Questionable players are conditional. The new opportunity estimates are not covered by the historical accuracy figures.

01Minutes & role

Estimate playing time from the last five games and season rotation. A changing role can help one player while another loses minutes.

02Opponent allowance

Compare opponents' per-36 role allowances with league norms; shrink small samples toward neutral.

03Injury impact

Fresh absences can change estimated minutes and per-minute production. Use observed with/without splits when available, or a capped role estimate when they are not. Long-standing absences are not boosted again.
